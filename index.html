The Space of a Larger Home, the Convenience of Whitefield

At Lodha Whitefield, residences from 2,500 sq. ft. provide space for family life, entertaining and relaxing on your private deck. Six high-rise towers are planned across 8.3 acres, with 80% open space.

Landscaped grounds, a grand clubhouse and Lodha’s approach to hospitality complement a home address minutes from Whitefield’s leading technology campuses. It brings your work, your family’s everyday needs and your time at home much closer together.
